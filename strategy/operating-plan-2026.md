# The O-Ring Store: Operating Plan, Now Through January 31

Built October 23, 2026, from NetSuite, HubSpot, the AP schedule, the FY 2026 income statement and balance sheets, and the 13-week cash forecast (`strategy/cash-plan/TORS_13-Week_Cash_Forecast.xlsx`).

## The goal

**Be whole by February 1.** Every bill paid within 15 days of its due date, payroll never missed, no new debt, and the business set up to grow in 2027.

**Where we are today:** about $63K in the bank and about $346K owed to vendors, $148K of it past due. Another $291K of purchase orders hasn't arrived yet, and there's $55K on the credit cards. The business makes money (about $354K in 2026 so far, probably closer to $270K once the payroll taxes are booked right). The cash went out the door in 2026: $106K of loans paid off early, $60K lent to Groundwork, $62K more inventory, and $37K of equipment and vehicles.

**What it takes:** **$97K a week in sales**, every week, through January 31. Planned at a 50% gross margin, with no new purchases beyond what's already ordered.

| Weekly sales | What it gets us by February 1 |
|---|---|
| Under $85K | Not enough. Backup plan kicks in (see "If this, then that") |
| **$97K** | **Whole by February 1. Cash never goes below zero.** |
| $101K | Whole, plus the credit cards paid off |
| $118K | Whole, with a $40K cushion the whole way |

The tightest stretch is **November 2 to 22**. That's when the most bills come due. Cash comes back in early December, then the open POs come due in December and January.

## Rules for everybody until February 1

1. **TORS cash stays in TORS.** No loans or advances to Groundwork, family, or anyone else.
2. **Payroll comes first.** Then payroll taxes and sales tax, then shipping carriers, then inventory vendors oldest first, then credit card minimums.
3. **No bill goes more than 15 days past its due date.**
4. **No new stock buys.** We only buy against an actual customer order. Exceptions go to Marty or Darion.
5. **Sell what's on the shelf.** Every dollar we sell from stock is cash we don't have to spend again.
6. **No new spending over $500 without Marty's OK.** That covers software, equipment, subscriptions, travel, and marketing.
7. **Monday numbers, every Monday.** Everyone knows where we stand.

## Operations, Warehouse and Shipping

**Job:** ship fast, ship right, and don't lose money on freight.

**Do:**
- **Ship every in-stock order the same day** if it's in by 2 PM. Speed is what we're known for, so protect it.
- **Check freight.** We charged customers $336K for shipping this year and paid about $319K plus fuel surcharges, so we roughly break even. Make sure web and quote shipping charges cover the real carrier cost, especially on heavy and overseas orders.
- **Count the top 200 fast movers** before Thanksgiving. If the system says we have it, we have to really have it, or the "no new buying" rule breaks.
- **Pull the dead stock** Purchasing picks for return or sale, and get it ready to go.
- **Keep ISO 9001 records current.** No shortcuts because things are busy.

**Weekly numbers:**

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Orders shipped same day | 95%+ |
| Shipping errors and reships | Track and drive down |
