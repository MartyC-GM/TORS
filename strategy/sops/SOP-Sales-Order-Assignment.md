# SOP: Sales Order Assignment and Protected Selling Time

The O-Ring Store LLC · Effective October 26, 2026 · Owner: Marty Frostad

# 1. Scope

This covers every sales order over $250, from any channel (website, phone, email, marketplace), and the daily inbound and proactive selling time for Marty, Janevea, Daro, Darion and Anson. It applies to new and existing customers.

# 2. Purpose

We are all sales first, customers first. Today a good customer can place a big order and nobody calls them. In 2026 so far, 48 new customers placed a first order of $1,000 or more, and 36 of them never ordered again. This SOP makes sure every order over $250 has one person's name on it, and every person has protected time to take care of their accounts and grow them.

# 4. Expectations

**Protected selling time, every working day:**

| Anson | Technical sales, his accounts | | As needed |

- **Inbound** means working the orders, quotes and calls that come to us, starting with the morning assignment list.
- **Proactive** means going after business that isn't calling us yet: first-time $1,000+ customers, the top accounts, quiet customers, and new OEMs.

Purchasing, accounting and everything else waits during selling time unless a customer is waiting on it.

**What "taking care of" an assigned order means:**
- Every assigned order over $250: the owner looks at it the same day or the next morning. Who is this customer, and is there more we can do for them?
- **First-time customer, order of $1,000 or more:** the owner calls within 7 business days. Thank them, ask what they build, and ask what else they buy. Then check in again at 30 days.
- **Existing account, order of $1,000 or more:** a short thank-you and check-in at least once a quarter.
- Log every call in HubSpot on the company record.
- Be honest, get them the price they need, and deliver what we promise. That's how Sophie grew Cabka from a web order into our biggest customer.

# 5. Procedure

1. **Order comes in over $250.** Whoever enters or reviews the sales order checks that the customer's Category is filled in.
2. **Assign the owner** from the table in section 3. If the customer's Sales Rep is TORS, set it to that owner (the morning task does this automatically). If the customer already has a named rep, leave it and work it out with them.
3. **Morning assignment list.** Each workday morning, each owner gets the list of yesterday's orders over $250 that are theirs, with first-time customers and orders over $1,000 flagged.
4. **Owner acts during selling time.** Review every order. Call every first-time $1,000+ customer within 7 business days, and log it in HubSpot.
5. **30-day check-in.** The owner follows up with every first-time $1,000+ customer 30 days after the first call.
6. **Uncategorized customers.** Daro tags the category. If it belongs to Marty and Janevea, Darion or Anson, he changes the Sales Rep and lets them know.
7. **House accounts.** Retail, individuals and no-company-name orders stay on TORS. No call is required, though anyone can call if something looks promising.

# 6. Review and Compliance

- **Monday meeting:** first-time $1,000+ customers in the last week, calls made, calls still open past 7 days, and how many have come back for a second order. Shown on the TORS Get-Whole Scorecard.
- **Target:** every first-time $1,000+ customer called within 7 business days. At least half of them reorder within 90 days (today it's about 1 in 4).
- **Monthly:** Marty reviews the selling-time commitments and the account split, and moves accounts if one person's load is too heavy.
- Review this SOP every quarter, or sooner if the assignment rules change.
