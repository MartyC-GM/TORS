# SOP: Expedite and Substitution

The O-Ring Store LLC · Effective October 26, 2026 · Owner: Marty Frostad

# 1. Scope

This covers any order a customer needs sooner than we can ship it, and any late order where we offer to expedite or substitute. It applies to every salesperson, purchasing, and the purchasing assistant/expediter.

# 2. Purpose

We can always expedite or substitute. It just costs money. This SOP makes it fast and clear who talks to the customer, who picks the fix, and who pays, so the customer gets an answer the same day instead of waiting while we figure out who decides.

# 3. Responsibilities

- **Salesperson on the account:** owns the conversation with the customer from start to finish. Takes the request, hands it to purchasing, and tells the customer the plan and the cost.
- **Purchasing / expediter (Autumn today):** picks the best fix (expedite, partial ship, or substitute), gets the cost and new date, and approves it under the rules below.
- **Marty:** approves anything over the limit.

# 4. Expectations

**Who pays:**

| Situation | Who pays |
|---|---|
| The customer asks for it sooner than we promised | **The customer.** Quote them the cost before we place the expedite. |
| We messed up (late, wrong part, our miss) | **TORS pays.** Purchasing can approve up to **25% of the order total**. Over 25% goes to Marty. |

- When we're paying, pick the cheapest fix that still meets the customer's need. A substitute or partial ship is often cheaper than air freight.
- Same-day answer. The customer hears back from the salesperson the same business day the request comes in.
- Never expedite on the customer's dime without their OK on the price first.

# 5. Procedure

1. **Customer asks for it sooner** (a call, an email, or a reply to the late-order update). The salesperson logs it in HubSpot and sends it to purchasing with the order number, what's needed, and the date the customer needs it.
2. **Purchasing finds the options:** expedite from the vendor, ship what's on hand now, or substitute an equivalent part. Purchasing gets the cost and the new date for each one.
3. **Decide who pays:**
   - Customer asked for it: give the salesperson the cost to quote.
   - Our miss: purchasing approves it if the cost is 25% of the order total or less. If it's more than 25%, purchasing sends it to Marty with the options.
4. **Salesperson tells the customer** the plan, the date, and the cost if they're paying. Get their OK in writing (email reply is fine) before purchasing places a customer-paid expedite.
5. **Purchasing places it** and updates the expected ship date on the sales order.
6. **Charge it.** Customer-paid expedites go on the sales order as an expedite or freight line. TORS-paid expedites are noted on the order (memo: "Expedite, TORS paid, reason ...") so we can track what our misses cost.

# 6. Review and Compliance

- Monday meeting: number of expedites last week, customer-paid vs. TORS-paid, and total TORS-paid cost.
- Monthly: Marty reviews TORS-paid expedites to find the misses that keep repeating (vendor, item, or process) and fix the root cause.
- Review this SOP every quarter.
